# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate,
give constructive feedback, respect differing experience and viewpoints, and
focus disagreements on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
language or attention, publishing another person's private information, and
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
standard.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security tab. Do not
open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. Maintainers will
handle reports confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest when assigning a
reviewer.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project.
