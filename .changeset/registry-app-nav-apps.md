Add the optional `appBar` flag to `AppLink` (set to `false` on Comunidad), `APP_BAR_ORDER` (the order of the apps in the shell's app bar), `sortAppBarApps`, and `APP_BAR_APPS`: the apps shown in the shell's app bar, sorted by `APP_BAR_ORDER`. `APPS` keeps its order and every entry keeps its data.
