Add the staff navigation as an opt-in mode of `AppShell`: `navigation="unified"` (the default `'launcher'` is unchanged). The header shows a way back («← App») to the page you left in the app you came from, the host `start`, a «Buscar o ir a…» search field (⌘K / Ctrl+K, or «/» when focus is not in a field), and the apps grid where it sits in launcher mode. The sidebar shows «Mis apps» under the brand, fed by the new optional `preferences.favoriteApps` and `setFavoriteApps` of the staff-shell context (hidden when the host provides neither); the staff-shell snapshot gains an optional `preferences.favoriteApps`. A grid tile or a «Mis apps» row opens the last page used in that app and carries the way back. The mode makes the brand open the start page of the current app, drops the pinned Nexus item, remembers your last page per app in two small cookies, and reports moves through the new `onNavigate` prop (`source`: `'launcher' | 'favorites' | 'goto' | 'wayback' | 'brand' | 'menu'`). New `appSub` and `navMemoryIgnore` props, a `useShellNavigation()` hook, an optional `pages` index on the staff-shell context, and `APP_NAV_APPS` re-exported from the registry.
